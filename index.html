A list of who needs you, not a grid of everyone

Most monitoring products show you all 240 patients and leave triage to your staff. MedPull inverts that. Patients are ranked by clinical concern, each with the reason written out, so the first thing your team reads is the decision they need to make.

  • Silence counts as a signal. A patient who stops responding surfaces like one reporting worsening pain.
  • Compared to the expected curve. Progress is judged against the recovery path for that procedure, not a generic baseline.
  • On-track patients stay quiet. No attention spent on the patients recovering as planned.

RTM revenue without the RTM paperwork

Remote Therapeutic Monitoring is reimbursable, but most practices skip it because tracking data days and interactive minutes by hand isn't worth the trouble. MedPull counts both automatically, as a byproduct of work your team already does.

  • Thresholds tracked live. See which patients hit 16 days of data and 20 interactive minutes, and which are close.
  • Documentation attached. Each code exports with the check-ins and clinician time that substantiate it.
  • Compliance guardrails. Codes surface as ready only once the requirements are met.

RTM code availability and reimbursement vary by payer and setting. MedPull supports your billing workflow and does not provide billing or legal advice.
